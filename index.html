Accuracy & privacy

This is an estimate for field planning, not a legal land survey. Satellite imagery can be offset, GPS accuracy varies with phone, weather and tree cover, and boundaries should be checked on the ground. Location is used only while you use the app; saved records remain in local browser storage. No ads or analytics are included in this app code.
